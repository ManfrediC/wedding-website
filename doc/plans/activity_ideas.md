Ideas for activities in Zurich / Switzerland in general

# Zurich
- hike or take the train up Uetliberg
- Kunsthaus Zürich (mention the type of art that is found there)
- Museum Rietberg
- Swim in Lake and Limmat (note that current is strong in Limmat, only for confident swimmers)
- Walk or run along Limmat
- Lindt chocolate factory and museum (fun for children)
- Boat excursion of Lake Zurich

# Day trips
- Lucerne including boat on Vierwaldstättersee
- Alpstein: reachable as day trip, beautiful hikes for all levels of ability
- Rheinfall
- Bern: attractive old town
- Basel: old town, notable for art museums (Fondation Beyeler, Kunstmuseum Basel)

# Further away
- Swiss national park in Graubünden
- Lauterbrunnen with Jungfraujoch
- Hikes: 5 Seenwanderung (pizol), Aletschgletscher, many options in Berner Oberland, Graubünden, Valais
