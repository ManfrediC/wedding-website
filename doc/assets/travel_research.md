# Travel Research Notes

Researched on 2026-05-10 for the wedding website travel page. June 2027 flight and ferry schedules are not fully bookable yet, so route details are planning guidance and should be rechecked closer to booking.

## Chicago

- Main route: Chicago O'Hare (ORD) to Zurich (ZRH).
- Current SWISS published schedules show nonstop Chicago-Zurich flights, including SWISS and United-operated services.
- Source: <https://www.swiss.com/lhg/us/en/o-d/cy-cy/chicago-zurich>

## New York

- Main routes: JFK-ZRH and EWR-ZRH.
- Current SWISS published schedules show nonstop New York-Zurich flights from JFK and Newark, including United-operated Newark services.
- Delta also publishes Zurich flight information and should be compared from New York when dates are available.
- Sources:
  - <https://www.swiss.com/lhg/us/en/o-d/cy-cy/new-york-zurich>
  - <https://www.delta.com/us/en/flight-deals/europe-flights/flights-to-zurich>

## London and the UK

- Main route: direct London-Zurich flights, especially from Heathrow, Gatwick, and London City.
- EasyJet can be budget-friendly, especially from Gatwick and other London airports, but the lowest fare may include only a small under-seat cabin bag.
- Current easyJet cabin-bag guidance says all customers get one small under-seat cabin bag, currently 45 x 36 x 20 cm including handles and wheels, while a large cabin bag must be included in the fare, membership, seat choice, or added booking.
- Rail option: Eurostar from London to Paris, transfer across Paris, then TGV Lyria to Zurich. Eurostar publishes the London-Zurich rail journey as about 7 h 25 min.
- Sources:
  - <https://www.swiss.com/lhg/gb/en/o-d/cy-cy/london-zurich>
  - <https://www.easyjet.com/en/flights-from-london/to-zurich/>
  - <https://www.easyjet.com/en/help/preparing-to-fly/baggage>
  - <https://www.eurostar.com/uk-en/train/london-to-zurich>

## Sardinia

- Focus on Sardinia for Italian guests, especially Cagliari (CAG) and Olbia (OLB).
- Edelweiss publishes seasonal Zurich-Cagliari and Zurich-Olbia timetables. Its timetable page says flights can be booked 361 days before departure, so June 2027 needs later confirmation.
- EasyJet can be budget-friendly for Olbia-Zurich when available, with the same strict baggage caveat as London routes.
- Driving option: overnight ferry from Sardinia to Genoa, then drive Genoa-Zurich.
- GNV publishes Genoa-Porto Torres at about 12 h 30 min direct.
- Moby publishes Genoa-Olbia at about 10-13 h, with vehicle check-in timing.
- Drivers need to plan the Swiss motorway vignette through the official Swiss portal and allow for Alpine tunnel traffic.
