# Accessibility Research Notes

Research checkpoint: 2026-05-11.

These notes support the public-facing accessibility and mobility card on the Stay page. They are not a substitute for direct confirmation with the venue or accommodation before booking.

## Wedding Venues And Transport

- Kirche St. Peter official FAQ says the ground floor of the church is wheelchair-accessible. It describes a signposted lift from St. Peterhofstatt to the church, and states that the gallery and choir area are not wheelchair-accessible. It also lists a hearing loop.
- The Reformierte Kirche Zurich room platform also marks Kirche St. Peter and Kirchplatz St. Peter as wheelchair-accessible.
- ZSG/ZVV accessibility information says Lake Zurich boats can carry wheelchairs up to 80 cm wide in 2nd class, with crew assistance at landing stages. It notes exceptions and limitations for Limmat river boats, and says the exact boat/station setup matters.
- The wedding boat, boarding point, and route are still provisional, so the website should not promise final step-free access until those details are confirmed with the operator.

## Accommodation

- Hotel Sonne room pages vary by room category. Several room pages state that not all rooms in that category are accessible by lift, while at least one room page describes the room as accessible and reachable by lift. Guests needing lift access should contact the hotel before booking.
- Zurich Tourism lists Boutiquehotel Sonne with elevators, but individual room category access still needs direct confirmation.
- OXEN publishes that its seven rooms are on the top floor and have shared bathrooms. No clear official accessibility statement was found during this checkpoint.
- B & B Caffettino-Vino states that the old house has no lift and rooms are on the 2nd and 3rd floors.
- Zurich hotels and Airbnb apartments vary by property. Airbnb has accessibility filters, but guests should confirm step-free access, bathroom access, lift dimensions, and public-transport distance directly with the property.
